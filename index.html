Methodology & Data Classification Framework

Observed / Estimated Values

Baseline estimates for 2019 (e.g. 55.2M cases, $1.3T global cost) derived from WHO epidemiological and economic modeling.

WHO Projections

2030 and 2050 epidemiological numbers based on static age-specific prevalence combined with UN UNWPP population projections.

Calculated / Adjusted Values

Cost figures adjusted for projected real growth in long-term care wage and support costs (e.g. $2.8T scenario for 2030).

Illustrative Scenarios

Interactive simulations (e.g. risk reduction delayed onset) designed to demonstrate analytical impact, not formal WHO forecasts.

Source: World Health Organization. Global status report on the public health response to dementia. 2021. ISBN 978-92-4-003324-5.

Global Cost Composition (WHO Report Data)

Informal care accounts for roughly ~50% of the $1.3 Trillion global cost burden.

Cost Distribution by World Bank Income Group

Economic Inequality: In Low- and Middle-Income Countries (LMICs), up to 65% of total dementia costs are borne through unpaid informal caregiving. In High-Income Countries (HICs), formal social care and direct medical costs make up a significantly higher proportion.
